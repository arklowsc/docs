The **GCP managed instance group** target connects Arklow to an existing Google Cloud autoscaler. Arklow commands one replica count by setting the autoscaler's minimum and maximum to that count.

## Configuration

Along with the [shared group fields](/resources/scale-targets/gcp/index#shared-configuration), enter the **Autoscaler** name attached to the managed instance group.

The target's **Minimum replicas** and **Maximum replicas** are permission bounds on Arklow's commands. For each command, Arklow writes the same count to the autoscaler's `minNumReplicas` and `maxNumReplicas` settings.

This target supports replica counts of 1 or more. It cannot scale the group to zero.

## Required permissions

The selected GCP Service Account credential needs:

```text
compute.instanceGroupManagers.get
compute.autoscalers.get
compute.autoscalers.update
```

You can place these permissions in a custom role scoped to the project containing the group.

## Add an autoscaler target

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable managed instance groups">
    Open the GCP Service Account credential and enable **Google Cloud managed instance groups** under **Scale target providers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync the inventory">
    Click **Sync now** and open [**Scale Targets**](https://app.arklow.io/dashboard/scale-targets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the group">
    Select the managed instance group under **Provider inventory** and click **Attach**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select capacity">
    Choose the destinations served by the group, or an existing pool. Choosing destinations creates a new pool with those destinations as its members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set permission bounds">
    Enter the lowest and highest replica counts Arklow may command.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach">
    Attach the target and confirm that the observed target size belongs to the expected group.
  </Step>
</Steps>
