The Together AI target commands the replica count of one dedicated endpoint through its autoscaling settings.

## Configuration

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Endpoint ID** | The dedicated endpoint Arklow may observe and change. |
| **Credential** | An [HTTP Auth Headers credential](/resources/credentials/http/auth-headers). |
| **Minimum replicas** | The lowest replica count Arklow may command. Together AI requires at least `1`. |
| **Maximum replicas** | The highest replica count Arklow may command. |

Use the endpoint ID shown in Together AI, such as `endpoint-abcd1234`.

## Authentication

Create an HTTP Auth Headers credential with the bearer token used for the Together AI API:

```text
Authorization: Bearer <api-key>
```

## Observations and changes

Arklow reads the endpoint state and configured autoscaling bounds. When the minimum and maximum match, Arklow treats that value as the exact configured count. If they differ, Arklow uses the minimum as a lower bound and keeps the target unsettled. The target therefore reports reduced observability.

Arklow commands a count by setting the endpoint's minimum and maximum replicas to that value. Arklow pauses changes while the endpoint is `STOPPED`; start the endpoint in Together AI to resume scaling.

## Attach a Together AI target

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Together AI">
    Open the HTTP Auth Headers credential and enable **Together AI** under **Scale target providers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync the inventory">
    Click **Sync now** and open [**Scale Targets**](https://app.arklow.io/dashboard/scale-targets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the endpoint">
    Select the endpoint under **Provider inventory** and click **Attach**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select capacity">
    Choose the destinations served by this endpoint, or an existing pool. Choosing destinations creates a new pool with those destinations as its members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set permission bounds">
    Enter the lowest and highest replica counts Arklow may command. The minimum must be at least `1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach">
    Attach the target and confirm that **Last observed** advances and **Health** shows no credential or endpoint error.
  </Step>
</Steps>
